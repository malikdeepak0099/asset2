Visa

UAE citizens can visit Georgia without a visa. Residents of the UAE with another nationality may be able to enter visa-free with a valid UAE residence permit, depending on their passport. Check the official rules for your passport before you travel. You can also buy without travelling, using a power of attorney.

How to buy, step by step

  1. Tell us your budget on WhatsApp. An agent sends a shortlist of matching apartments the same day.
  2. Remote viewing. A KEY GROUP agent walks you through the apartment and the building on a live WhatsApp video call, and sends extra photos and videos. You can also fly in for a viewing.
  3. Checks. We pull the official Public Registry (NAPR) extract to confirm the owner and any mortgage or restriction before you pay anything.
  4. Agree terms and sign. Sign in person in Georgia, or remotely: give a trusted representative a power of attorney, notarised and apostilled (or legalised) in your country.
  5. Pay and register. Ownership is registered at the Public Service Hall. Official NAPR fee: 150 GEL (4 working days), 270 GEL (next working day) or 350 GEL (same day). There is no stamp duty.
  6. After the purchase. We can help you rent the apartment out, or apply for a residence permit if the property qualifies.

Rules for foreign buyers (checked November 2026)

  • Ownership: foreigners can buy apartments, houses and commercial property on the same terms as Georgian citizens. Agricultural land is restricted for foreigners.
  • Residence permit: since 1 March 2026, owning non-agricultural property with a certified market value of at least US$150,000 can qualify you for a one-year, renewable temporary residence permit. An investment of US$300,000 or more can qualify for an investment residence permit. A property-based permit does not by itself give the right to work in Georgia.
  • Property tax: since the October 2026 Tax Code change, families whose annual income does not exceed GEL 100,000 pay no property tax on apartments and houses (land is taxed separately). Above that, the rate is 0.8–1% of market value.
  • Rent and resale: residential rental income of individuals is taxed at 5%. Selling a home you have owned for more than 2 years is generally not taxed in Georgia.
