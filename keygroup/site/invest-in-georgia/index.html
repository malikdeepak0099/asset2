Can foreigners buy property in Georgia?

Yes. Foreigners can buy apartments, houses and commercial property with just a passport, with the same rights as locals; agricultural land is restricted.

How long does registration take?

Usually 1–4 working days at the Public Service Hall, at a cost of about 50–350 GEL depending on speed. There is no stamp duty.
